Why did chocolate ice cream taste bitter?<br>
<br>
These hands were trembling<br>
"Let me tell you about my day", she said<br>
I laughed, they suddenly stopped<br>
<br>
Sumotaro-chan was looking at me<br>
He slept on the floor next to the old couple's car<br>
His eyes sparked, "Come on"<br>
<br>
I took the wrong bus again<br>
then pressed the red button for the next stop<br>
And there they were, a flower field, and a huge rainbow<br>
<br>
They played 'River Flows in You' at the 300 years old bonsai tree garden<br>
I heard, "Everything to do with this affair is in God's hands"<br>
And suddenly it was pouring<br>
