Hi again, keep going, my love.<br>
<br>
I know, I miss you too. I am sorry I experienced another love before you. I won't mind if you did too.<br>
<br>
I have chosen restraint so much that this overflowing affection would go to waste if we don't meet each other in this world. I hope we do.<br>
<br>
I honestly can't wait to meet your beautiful mind and heart. I wonder how different we think and process emotions. Of course, I won't mind if you sleep halfway through my yap about theology, philosophy, politics, economy, or anything.<br>
<br>
I miss you now. I hope you are doing well. I hope we can finally find each other.<br>
<br>
I will write to you everytime this overflowing affection has nowhere to go now. Yes, my love, you will have a book full of letters on our first night, haha.
