"Did you see the Hibiscus flower? It is blooming again after being dormant during the winter", she said, while pouring cold barley tea in my little cup.<br>
<br>
Perhaps, it was that weekend workday, she said that she just did a chemo. My tears suddenly broke and I could not stand up. We hugged and cried, she was the one reassuring me that she would get better.<br>
<br>
And she wishes that my heart will bloom again, for how I have lived and loved.
