Evening, ma'am. I bet you are dreaming in your sleep now.<br>
<br>
I have one and half hour to write a letter before archiving this blog forever. I began writing again more than a year ago. It was an act to confront myself, to face the hard things after our first conversation last year.<br>
<br>
I find my purpose. I know who I want to become. I understand how to build it.<br>
<br>
Mom, I also loved a boy, a man. As you said, it is partly true, we love someone because it is that person, we can't reason through it.<br>
<br>
Mom, we decided to part ways, not because we loved each other less, but because we loved each other enough to wish the best.<br>
<br>
I learned a lot about what it means to be a woman, a wife, a mother, a best friend through you. You are the best thing that happens in my life too. Thank you for raising me to be this person. If there are multiple universes, I will choose you as my mother in each one of them.<br>
<br>
Mom, I have seen enough kindness, resilience, independence, warmth, love from you. I am trying to do what you taught by paying it forward, to the people I loved, love, will love.<br>
<br>
Mom, I remember everything. I wish I could tell you that I have moved past it, that I finally forgive Dad, but I don't want to reopen an old scar.<br>
<br>
Of course, I love you three times more than I love Dad. But Mom, I love Dad too. Thank you for understanding it when I was finally able to put the feelings into words.<br>
<br>
I remember when I was in elementary school, you used to keep this little journal in your phone's memo. I do not remember the contents, but I remember that they were heartbreaking pieces. I am glad that you found writing as a friend, like I do.<br>
<br>
Mom, it is my dream to write about you someday. I hope the dream comes true.<br>
<br>
The blog was started because of you. I finished it because of you too.<br>
<br>
You have always been my first love, I love you.
