The world is a scary and cold place, isn't it? I can see it clearly in your eyes, the way you see things. You are one of the strongest people I have ever met. I do not believe that a person needs other people to rescue themselves. I believe that you have done the rescuing, to yourself, very well. <br>
<br>
I should have let you talk more clearly, not in a one-sentence outburst. I should have let you finish your coffee. I should have been a better friend. I want to sit next to you and tell you that you are going to live a wonderful life, that you are going to conquer the world and live your dreams, but I hope someone will do that to you instead.
