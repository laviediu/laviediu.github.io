The world did not stop. My work did not care what my heart went through. I moved from one chair to another, hoping the next chair would soothe the pain. I tried to stand, but my heart was too heavy, so I squatted. I let the tears wet the dusty floor. I shouted the pain away, hoping it would make my chest less heavy. Someone walked in, I hide behind the chair, "I am sorry I am crying now". I apologized for crying in the place where I work, where I should stand tall. Perhaps, it is the pain. Perhaps, it is my soul becoming more sensitive at recognizing another pain. I saw the pain in their eyes. I became curious, so I paid attention to the hidden clues, in their steps and voices. And suddenly, the ache in my chest becomes lighter, the tears in my eyes becomes colder. I am not alone.
