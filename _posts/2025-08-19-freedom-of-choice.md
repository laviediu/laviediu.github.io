Is it because I am almost 27? I have always liked the number 27, it was my student number in elementary school. I wonder what kind of profession did I want to do back then. I think it was teacher, which is quite predictable for someone like me. I do like teaching. I feel like I have always been throwing myself to what life gives me. Or perhaps, I have been following what society expects me to follow.<br>
<br>
Perhaps, I have been looking at life differently ever since I cut off the only safety net I had a couple years ago. Perhaps, because I choose to be my own person, to follow what makes me feel personally fulfilled. Perhaps, because I do not have responsibilities over siblings or children or partner. Or in other words, perhaps, because I am privileged to be alone.<br>
<br>
Mom texted me that she was just given a bigger responsibility at her workplace. "I am very proud of you, I hope you can do it well", I replied. I remember vividly how she sacrificed her dreams to take care of me. Not that she resented it, she seems to love me more than her own life. I still don't understand it. "You will know how it feels when you have your own children", she said. I don't know Mom, what if I choose another road?<br>
<br>
Life is funny. No one predicted that COVID-19 will happen at the end of 2019. No one predicted the one AI paper which birthed a massive development of everything that follows.<br>
<br>
Like Mom who pursues her dreams when she is almost 50, perhaps I could do mine then. Or perhaps, I could just do it tomorrow. Because I am not scared of jumping off a cliff anymore. And wherever I land, I know that God is the only safety net I have at the end of the day.
