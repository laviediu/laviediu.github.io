I finished my measurement in Yokohama at almost 11 pm. The train was delayed for more than 20 minutes. I was quite surprised at myself because I was not panicking, "I will probably stay at the nearest hotel then", I thought. Fortunately, the JR service in Tokyo is very accommodating. I thought I won't get the last train, but they added several trains with the last stop a station away from the station nearest to my place. I walked for about 15 minutes and arrived home at 1 am.<br>
<br>
During my walk home, I have this strange feelings. I did not feel the burning anger in the chest. I think it was an utter disbelief and a deep disappointment. As soon as I sat in front of my desk, I wrote a piece as sharp as samurai blade.<br>
<br>
*Curses are too grand. The audacity to be a coward. This piece does not deserve to exist.*<br>
<br>
It was actually longer and sharper. I felt like I shot a person in the heart without hesitation. I was laughing after writing it. I was talking to Allah whether I was allowed to feel it, whether it was a sin to even write it for myself. No one reads my blog except my future self, but it felt terribly sinful. Not because of how sharp it was, but because of whether I was allowed to express the emotions at all.<br>
<br>
I remember the day when my traumatic experience was triggered. My nervous system was in high alert, I could not think straight. Looking back, I felt like I was under some sort of magical illusion, haha. And I just realized that trauma triggers are inevitable for people suffering traumatic experiences.<br>
<br>
My best friend asked, "What kind of words of affirmation work for you?". I replied, "I don't know, I don't think I even need words of affirmation as much as having someone to sit next to me when the scary things happen". I added, "You don't need to ask me anything, you should never ask me to understand anything logically because I am in literal pain, you can talk about anything else while I am processing my own pain". I was like, huh, I understand why I have a deep disappointment towards the person. The person was partly doing the opposite of what I needed.<br>
<br>
To be fair, trauma triggers will never seem reasonable to anyone except the person experiencing the trauma itself. I have this friend who was always suspicious of her neighbors because she felt like they stalked her. When in fact, they did not. Also, it was my first time experiencing the horrible episode. I did not know what to do either, haha.<br>
<br>
Perhaps because Dad always flew from the scene whenever conflicts happened, or when things got hard. Or perhaps because Dad would snap with words or slam something. When honestly, he could just sit there, not to repair anything, but to not leave Mom by herself.<br>
<br>
It has been a very long journey from feeling like I should not exist in this world, feeling like a broken person, feeling like I broke someone I loved, to finally forgive my own self and move forward.<br>
<br>
Ah I need to see my therapist again at the end of this year. Also, probably a medical check-up this month. I think something is weird with my heart, I had this one episode that could be a heart attack but I hope it wasn't. The symptoms that I often have are typically just a shortness of breath and pressure on the left chest, but there was one episode where the pain went further to other parts of my body, which is quite scary. I guess the additional chest heaviness from heartbreak did something to the original symptoms that I have.<br>
<br>
I will end this heavy piece with good memories here. I recommended two of my good friends for an internship at the company where I used to work!!! I am very happy for them because I know how much they need it, I hope they get the position. Also, my friends and I went to a startup exhibition. We met this CEO who just launched his startup last year, with a very clever solution of offering heavy machinery part repairs through additive manufacturing process. We ended the day with Hainan chicken and soft-shelled crabs!<br>
<br>
Thank you, Maryam Medina, for giving yourself the permission to feel human feelings, whatever they are, haha.
