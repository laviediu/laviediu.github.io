The world did not stop. My work did not care what I went through. I moved from one chair to another, hoping the next chair would soothe the pain. I tried to stand, but my heart was too heavy, so I squatted. I let the tears wet the dusty floor. I shouted the pain away, hoping it would make my chest less heavy.<br>
<br>
Someone walked in, I hid behind the chair, "I am sorry I am crying now". I apologized for crying in the place where I should stand tall. Perhaps, it is the pain. Perhaps, it is my soul becoming more sensitive at recognizing another pain. I saw the pain in their eyes. <br>
<br>
I became curious, so I paid attention to the hidden clues, in their steps and voices. And suddenly, the ache in my chest became lighter, the tears in my eyes became colder. I am not alone.
