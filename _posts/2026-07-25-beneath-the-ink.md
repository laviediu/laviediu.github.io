title: "beneath the ink"
date: 2026-07-25
author: "Maryam Medina"
categories: poem
---


I see you in new places I visit, new people I meet.<br>
I write thousands of words, only to discover you beneath the ink.<br>
I rest my thoughts, but you follow me in the dreams.<br>
I wake to the morning, and you are still there, aching in my heart.<br>
<br>
I ask God to erase a hope that I do not hold tightly.<br>
I ask God to take this love from me.<br>
But am I dictating God's decree?<br>
<br>
A stranger, we are now.<br>
I plead to Him to keep you better without me.<br>
