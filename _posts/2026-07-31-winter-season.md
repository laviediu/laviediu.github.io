Change happens quickly, and bad memories outlast the good ones. I closed my eyes, I asked the chatbot assistant to read it for me. I read it once, as the words already rang in my ears. As if writing in italic would let me see the words clearer, make the band-aid rips faster. As if logical reasoning would make the wound smaller, turn the pain lesser. As if being generous would make me negotiate the terms, misunderstand the decision. And just like that, I gathered my strength through the soaking cheeks, swallowed my pains, walked away without adding burdens. I put the train receipts that I kept in the wallet in a box, deep inside the shelf I rarely open. Perhaps, the ice is what makes the wound from burning. Perhaps, indifference is found in coldness. And perhaps, silence is what makes the burial ground from turning to goodbye celebration.
