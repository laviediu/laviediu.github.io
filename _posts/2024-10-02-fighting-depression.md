Is this depression? I always think it’s all my fault, my fault for not trying harder. My fault for choosing the wrong choice. My fault for even existing.<br>
<br>
I can’t focus on my work. I was crying all day a week ago for leaving my family. My eyes hurt when I woke up the next morning. I went to the lab on Friday to take care of my paperworks. I was getting rest on weekend. My body was aching all over from the long flight. And I found out that my fellowship was getting rejected. I was processing on how to handle the situation. I can survive here, although I won’t add more funds to the saving. I have been thinking to get a job if a year won’t work here.<br>
<br>
Then yesterday mom and dad was having a fight, again. I am so tired of it all. I said to her that it is okay if she wants to get a divorce. I said that I am an adult now and I know that there is an expiration date to a relationship. Then she said that she didn’t mean it.<br>
<br>
I don’t see love between them anymore. Except once, when I asked my dad to escort me to my friend’s wedding. Mom said she wanted to come because dad will be all alone in the parking lot. And once, when dad asked me to take my mom from work in the evening.<br>
<br>
God I don’t even believe in marriage anymore. My heart aches and my head hurts. I can’t stop tears flowing through my cheeks. I hate that I can’t go to work like this. I can’t write my paper although the idea is already all there in my brain. I hate that I have no one comforting me during this difficult situation. I know I have Allah.<br>
<br>
But God please please please, is adulting supposed to be this difficult? Is my sin too much that I can’t bear to live? I also don’t want to die, I can’t, I know I have many things I should do. But God, oh God, can I ask You the favor of a peaceful week?<br>
<br>
I am so sick of myself. I hate myself. I hate all the people who said that they love me but never be there for me. I wonder if this would ever stop.
