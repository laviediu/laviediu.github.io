Hi, I hope you are doing good.<br>
<br>
They said that women process heartbreak through trying to understand the situation. I think there is some truth to that. I understand our situation, I understand yours and mine. This is my first heartbreak, I hope it is my last. It was deeply hurtful, but it was also beautiful. To experience heartbreak means to experience love.<br>
<br>
Heartbreak change people, for the better. You loved me for who I was, I loved you for who you were.<br>
<br>
While the wound is still fresh, I am completely sure that when I look back to this chapter 10 years from now, I would smile. I keep the Shinkansen receipt and the last poem, I think I will store them safe in my parents' house. If I ever have a daughter, I will tell her about you. I am grateful that I was once loved by someone like you.<br>
<br>
There are a lot of bad memories at the end, aren't there? If I could choose one moment to freeze, I would keep the one where we bowed to each other after the long walk, when we told each other our dreams. Yours was a gift, mine was a sign.<br>
<br>
We loved each other, hurt each other. There is nothing to forgive, and I hope you have forgiven me as well.<br>
<br>
I wish you the most beautiful future. I wish you meet someone who paints the life you want to sketch. I wish you be kinder to yourself. I wish you see the good in things.<br>
<br>
And in case you are wondering, while I will not love you for the rest of my life, I will love your mom for the rest of my life. You are not alone in wanting to see her again.<br>
<br>
Thank you for the beautiful chapter. I loved you too.
